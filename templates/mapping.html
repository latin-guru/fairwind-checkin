Consent is more than a property. In production a ticked box also subscribes the contact to the marketing email subscription type (Communication Preferences API, statusState=SUBSCRIBED, legalBasis=CONSENT_WITH_NOTICE), and an opt-out sends UNSUBSCRIBED. hs_email_optout and hs_marketable_status are set by HubSpot itself; this app only reads them. The preferred intake is the Forms API with legalConsentOptions; the CRM batch upsert by email is the fallback. Both request shapes are in the sync log.
